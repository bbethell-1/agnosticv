*Red Hat One 2026*

Private LLM as a Service - A Practical Introduction with OpenShift AI - on AWS with LiteLLM

The environment is hosted on AWS.

In this hands-on session, participants will rotate between key personas critical to delivering modern, production-level AI services:
As a platform engineer participants will design and deploy model serving environments and prepare the infrastructure that makes scalable model hosting possible.
As an AI application developer, participants will connect real applications to private LLM endpoints, integrate code assistant technology to build and deploy fun, and real-world applications.
As a DevOps Practitioner, participants will then explore the capabilities of agentic AI to monitor your OpenShift cluster resources.
Lastly, as a technical decision maker, participants will see how to monitor and analyze your model usage across the cluster.
At the end, participants will walk away with practical experience across the lifecycle of model deployment.


Business Value: Accelerate and De-Risk the Enterprise Adoption of AI. This lab demonstrates a cohesive strategy to move from experimental AI to a production-grade, scalable, and governed AI operation. It bridges the gap between infrastructure, development, operations, and business leadership.

Business Objective: To provide a holistic, hands-on understanding of a complete AI service delivery platform, enabling participants to replicate a secure, efficient, and measurable AI practice within their own organizations, thereby driving innovation and operational excellence.
