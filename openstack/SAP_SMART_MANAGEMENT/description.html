Use Red Hat Satellite and Red Hat Insights for smart management of SAP workloads.

In this catalog item, you use Red Hat Satellite and Red Hat Insights for proactive monitoring, remediation, and life-cycle management of SAP landscapes.

Product(s): Red Hat Enterprise Linux for SAP Applications

About the Environment

  • You select the region from our three regular options.
  • Credentials and other relevant information are sent to you privately in an email after deployment is complete.
  • After you order your environment, you can manage it by logging into the system and selecting Services -> My Services -> [This service] -> App Control.
  • The ordered environment ceases to run unless you select Extend Runtime in App Control.
  • The ordered environment ceases to exist at the expiration date timestamp provided unless you select Extend Lifetime in App Control.
  • Deployments might fail randomly due to cloud errors. If you experience this, please try again.
