Provides a RHEL AI VM with an NVIDIA GPU and several pre-installed models for you to explore and experiment with.
You can choose the version of RHEL AI (currently limited to 1.5) and the GPU type.

== Products and Environment

* RHEL AI
* NVIDIA GPU
** g6.xlarge (1x L4 with 24GB GPU memory)
** g6.12xlarge (4x L4 with 96GB GPU memory)
* Models
** granite-3.1-8b-starter-v2
** granite-3.1-8b-lab-v2
** mixtral-8x7b-instruct-v0-1
** prometheus-8x7b-v2-0

[WARNING]
====
GPU instances are still very expensive and sometimes have limited availability.
Choose the smallest GPU instance that will work for your use case.

If you experience a deployment failure, please try provisioning again in a different region.
The *us-east-1* and *us-east-2* regions currently have the best capacity.
====
