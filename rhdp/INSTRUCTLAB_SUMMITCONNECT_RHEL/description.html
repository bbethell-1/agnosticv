Summit Connect 2024: RHEL AI and InstructLab - Get a taste in training your own model

Note: This workshop uses GPU resources and has a higher cost than normal. Please use economically and shutdown or suspend after use.

Workshop Overview

Welcome to the RHEL AI and InstructLab workshop at Red Hat Summit Connect 2024. In this hands-on session, you'll learn about RHEL AI and how to leverage it to improve a Large Language Model (LLM) using InstructLab.

What You'll Learn

  • Understanding Large Language Models (LLMs)
  • How LLMs are trained
  • Introduction to InstructLab and its innovative approach
  • Hands-on experience with InstructLab CLI
  • Integrating AI into a real-world application (Parasol Insurance)
  • Creating and modifying taxonomies
  • Generating synthetic data
  • Training and fine-tuning models

Workshop Structure

  1. Introduction to RHEL AI and InstructLab
  2. Setting up the environment
  3. Working with the InstructLab CLI
  4. Exploring the Parasol Insurance application
  5. Understanding and modifying taxonomies
  6. Generating synthetic data
  7. Training and interacting with the model
  8. Verifying improvements in the application

Resources

  • InstructLab Project
  • InstructLab Repo
  • InstructLab Taxonomy Repo

Environment Specifications

  • 1 x RHEL 9.4 VM with Nvidia GPU and CUDA
  • Python 3.11
  • Nvidia Drivers and CUDA pre-installed
  • Nvidia L4 GPU

Target Audience

This workshop is designed for AI Architects and Application Engineers interested in leveraging AI in their applications and understanding the process of fine-tuning LLMs.

Duration

The workshop is designed to be completed within the Summit Connect session time. Some steps, like extensive model training, will be simulated due to time constraints.

Get ready to dive into the world of AI and LLMs with RHEL AI and InstructLab!
