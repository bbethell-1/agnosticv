= LB1005: Day 2 Operations and Automation with OpenShift Virtualization

Learn how to efficiently manage and automate virtualized workloads in OpenShift using Ansible Automation Platform. This workshop focuses on Day 2 operational tasks—such as scaling, troubleshooting, and automation—giving platform engineers and VM administrators the hands-on experience they need to run resilient infrastructure at scale. Explore real-world scenarios, respond to incidents, and implement automation strategies that bring consistency and control to your environment.

*GitHub Pages:* https://rhpds.github.io/showroom-virt-aap-day-2

*Products*:

* Red Hat OpenShift 4.18.8
* Red Hat OpenShift Virtualization 4.18.2
* Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform 2.5

*Provisioning Time:* ~2 hours

*Ideal Audience:* Virtual Machine and Infrastructure Administrators looking to operationalize OpenShift Virtualization with automation best practices.
