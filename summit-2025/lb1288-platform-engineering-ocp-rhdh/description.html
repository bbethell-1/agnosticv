Red Hat One: Platform Engineering Workshop

The Platform Engineering Workshop is a multi module workshop which serves as a practical, learn-by-example style hands-on workshop, for Platform Engineers.

The workshop guides a Platform Engineer through a journey map of understanding why a platform is critical for increased developer productivity, and also learns to actually set one up right from Design phase to a more mature Product-as-as-Platform phase.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the need to implement Platform Engineering disciplines in their organization and see the benefits of improving Developer Productivity.
  • How to design a Thinnest Viable Platform (TVP) based on the needs of development teams in the organization and ensure they are highly productive.
  • Constantly assess the investment into your own IDP and understand how they can be enhanced, and convince development teams to start adopting it.
  • See Red Hat as a partner in their Platform Engineering journey through Red Hat's products, Professional Services, and Partner Ecosystem.

Modules overview

  • Module 1:Discovery
  • Module 2: Design the Internal Developer Platform
  • Module 3: Test drive Red Hat Developer Hub
  • Module 4: Discovery
  • Module 5: Setup Build, Test, & Deploy Outer-loop
  • Module 6: Establish Security and Compliance (Coming soon)
  • Module 7: Enable Continuous Platform Improvements (Coming soon)
