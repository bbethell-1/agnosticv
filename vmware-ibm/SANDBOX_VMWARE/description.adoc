Due to recent changes in how our cloud providers provide and support VMware environments, we can no longer maintain the level of reliability required for field use. Because of this, *we are fully retiring the VMware Open Environment on September 30, 2026.*

What does this mean for you?
* *Existing environments:* Will continue to run under our standard lifetime and support policies until September 30.
* *New environments:* No new provisions will be accepted after August 31, 2026.

_Note: This retirement only applies to the open sandbox environment. Narrative demos and workshops featuring specific VMware content are not affected._

If you have questions or concerns regarding this change, please open a support ticket to log your use case.
