= Rock, Scissors, Paper! An AI-powered interactive game with OpenShift AI and OpenShift GitOps (LB2259)

<p>Explore the exciting world of modern application development with our hands-on lab, "Rock, Scissors, Paper: An AI-powered interactive game with OpenShift AI and OpenShift GitOps." This comprehensive session will guide you through the process of deploying and upgrading models, pipelines, and more for the classic game of RoShambo, showcasing the capabilities of Red Hat OpenShift AI.</p>

<p>Throughout the lab, you will:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Learn how to deploy an AI model for the interactive game using Red Hat OpenShift AI & KServe Model Mesh</li>
  <li>Discover how data scientists can efficiently test and experiment with their models</li>
  <li>Visualize model automation using Red Hat OpenShift data science pipelines</li>
  <li>Utilize Red Hat OpenShift GitOps for streamlined applications deployment and updates</li>
  <li>Implement GitOps methodology for enhanced collaboration and automation</li>
</ul>

<p>The lab will demonstrate the synergy among OpenShift AI, OpenShift Pipelines, and OpenShift GitOps, highlighting their consistent collaboration for MLOps automation. Through practical demonstrations, you will witness the connectivity between models and applications, emphasizing the powerful combination of Red Hat OpenShift technologies for efficient MLOps practices.</p>

<p>This session is suitable for anyone interested in the integration of AI in IT projects and gaming. You will gain valuable insights into how Red Hat OpenShift technologies can streamline the development and deployment processes, enabling you to create engaging and interactive AI-powered applications.</p>

<p>Join us for this informative and hands-on lab to expand your knowledge of AI-powered gaming and discover how Red Hat OpenShift AI and OpenShift GitOps can revolutionize your development workflow.</p>
