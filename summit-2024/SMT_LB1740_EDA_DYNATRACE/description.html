Escape Room with EDA and Dynatrace

This demo is an introductory to EDA capabilities demonstrated with Dynatrace monitoring. It is purposed to give crowd a fun way of hunting info from both Dynatrace monitoring portal and AAP EDA and Controller portals.



We provision AAP with EDA having Dynatrace rulebook and decision environment with Dynatrace plugin. There needs to be RHEL VM somewhere with Dynatrace EasyTravel webshop and OneAgent running. Also it requires Dynatrace instance where OneAgent reports to, and EDA rulebook attaches to.

Once lab is commissioned we need to run a AAP job template given webshop url, Dynatrace url and token. This job will attach lab to Dynatrace counterparts.

Workshop is meant for escape room format, where people are grouped into 4-5 people groups. Each group gets a hint at the time, and they need to start hunting the right answers from both Dynatrace and AAP ends. After each right answer the group gets to next task. After solving all the tasks you get out of the room with lot of EDA understanding in your head :D
