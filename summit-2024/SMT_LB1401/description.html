= LB1401 - Deploying Red Hat Identity Management as a Lazy Sysadmin Deploying and managing Red Hat Identity Management does not have to be a difficult or long process. Using Ansible, whether it is Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform or Ansible Core which is included in RHEL. A couple of lazy sysadmins will demystify the installation and configuration of Identity Management systems for consistency and repeatability. From DR situations to managing trusts, or managing user and group access - the lazy sysadmin way will preserve the hair you have left.
