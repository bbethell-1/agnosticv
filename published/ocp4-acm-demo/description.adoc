This demo shows the five major use cases for Red Hat Advanced Cluster Management for Kubernetes: multicluster lifecycle, policy-driven governance
advanced application lifecycle, multicluster observability, and multicluster networking.
You will be provided an OpenShift cluster and AWS credentials.

== Products

* Red Hat Advanced Cluster Management for Kubernetes 2.17
* Red Hat OpenShift Container Platform 4.22
* Red Hat OpenShift Virtualization 4.22

== Demo Guide

You have full admin access to the provisioned workshop cluster and can find further details in the https://rhpds.github.io/showroom-rhacm-demo/modules/index.html[Demo guide].

== Demo Overview

=== Multicluster life cycle

* Centrally create, update, and delete Kubernetes clusters across multiple private and public clouds.
* Search, find, and modify any Kubernetes resource across the entire domain.
* Quickly troubleshoot and resolve issues across your federated domain.

=== OpenShift Virtualization

* Centrally manage virtual machines alongside containers across managed clusters from the ACM console.
* Create, start, stop, and migrate virtual machines on OpenShift clusters running OpenShift Virtualization.
* Apply governance policies to enforce virtual machine configuration standards and compliance across your fleet.
* Monitor virtual machine health and performance through multicluster observability dashboards.

=== Policy-driven governance

* Centrally set and enforce policies for security, applications, and infrastructure.
* Quickly visualize detailed auditing on the configuration of apps and clusters.
* Get immediate visibility into your compliance posture based on your defined standards.

=== Advanced application life cycle

* Easily deploy applications at scale.
* Deploy applications from multiple sources.
* Quickly visualize application relationships across clusters and those that span clusters.

=== Multicluster observability for health and optimization

* Work with Grafana to explore your cluster health leveraging the out-of-the-box dashboards.
* Create custom metrics and dashboards in Grafana.
* Navigate the out-of-the-box optimization dashboards.

=== Multicluster networking

* Leverage the Submariner open source project to connect multiple clusters.
* Explore and manage the networking resources for your clusters.
