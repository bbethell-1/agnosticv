Follow Parasol Insurance's journey from AI experimentation to production deployment. This hands-on lab walks developers through the full AI app development lifecycle — self-servicing a development environment from Red Hat Developer Hub, exploring AI model recipes with Podman Desktop's AI Lab extension, building and testing an AI-powered chatbot application, and deploying it to OpenShift through an automated CI/CD pipeline backed by GitLab and ArgoCD.

== Detailed Overview

* *Module 1 — Provisioning and Exploration*
** Self-service a fully configured RHEL 10 development VM through Red Hat Developer Hub
** Explore the Red Hat build of Podman Desktop and its extensions
** Use the AI Lab extension to experiment with AI models and recipes locally

* *Module 2 — Develop and Build*
** Clone and customize the Parasol Chat AI chatbot application
** Build and test the container image locally with Podman Desktop
** Push the container image and trigger the automated CI/CD pipeline via GitLab

* *Module 3 — Deploy and Verify*
** Observe the automated build and GitOps deployment through ArgoCD
** Verify the AI chatbot running live on OpenShift via the production pipeline
