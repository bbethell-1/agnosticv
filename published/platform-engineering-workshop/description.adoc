The Platform Engineering Roadshow is a multi module workshop which serves as a practical and learn-by-example style hands-on workshop for Platform Engineers.
The workshop guides a Platform Engineer through a journey map of understanding why a platform is critical for increased developer productivity and teaches how to
set one up right from design phase to a more mature Product-as-as-Platform phase.

== Learning Objectives

* Understand the need to implement Platform Engineering disciplines in their organization and see the benefits of improving Developer Productivity.
* How to design a Thinnest Viable Platform (TVP) based on the needs of development teams in the organization and ensure they are highly productive.
* Constantly assess the investment into your own IDP and understand how they can be enhanced, and convince development teams to start adopting it.
* See Red Hat as a partner in their Platform Engineering journey through Red Hat's products, Professional Services, and Partner Ecosystem.
