Architecture:

Agenda:

The demo consists of 4 independent segments. You can deliver the whole thing in 20 minutes, or go deep and deliver the segments in any order.

  • Segment 1: Stability and Speed Anywhere
    • Whether deployed via managed services or self-managed, Red Hat provides a consistent experience everywhere your applications run.
  • Segment 2: Application Modernization and Developer Experience
    • Remove toil from developers' inner and outer loops using a powerful, but familiar, set of open source tools for developing, testing, modernizing, and deploying applications.
  • Segment 3: Manage and Secure at Scale
    • Policy-based rules enforcement facilitates quick deployment of clusters across distributed locations to provide availability, capacity, and security.
  • Segment 4: Extend to the Edge
    • Applications extend to the edge, so does your sphere of management. Ansible and Red Hat Enterprise Linux extend beyond the data center and cloud.
