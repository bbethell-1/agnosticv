Learn about automating the enterprise to address challenges due to lack of automation.

Common challenges include the following:

  • Manual processes pose security risks, increase costs, and create inefficiencies.
  • The lack of automation standards, governance, and management strategy across disparate teams and tools makes it difficult to scale best practices.
  • Reduction in IT budgets presents challenges as companies must move towards maximizing efficiency with minimal resources, making it difficult to drive innovation.
