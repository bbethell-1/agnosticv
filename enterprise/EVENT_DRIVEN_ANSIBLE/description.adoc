This demonstration highlights the versatility of Event-driven Ansible. Through Ansible rulebooks, customers can see how automated actions can be initiated by a diverse range of events, tailored to their specific use cases. In this demo, OpenShift is an example of a possible event trigger, emphasizing that event-driven Ansible is not bound to a single platform. Instead, it is a flexible tool designed to optimize operations across many environments. Provided in a straightforward and digestible format, this demonstration unveils the potential of adaptive automation. It illustrates how event-driven Ansible can reinvent automated workflows, allowing customers to envisage the practical benefits in their unique contexts. Use this demonstration as a compelling tool to guide your customers toward understanding the advanced capabilities and advantages of event-driven Ansible. It's an invaluable opportunity to present the future of automation to them.
