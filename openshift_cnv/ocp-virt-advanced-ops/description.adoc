Late-in sales cycle workshop. Advanced operational tasks — such as scaling, troubleshooting, and automation.

This hands-on workshop presents advanced operations topics in OpenShift Virtualization.
It is designed to give platform engineers and VM administrators the hands-on experience they need to run resilient infrastructure at scale.
In it, they explore real-world scenarios, respond to incidents, and implement automation strategies that bring consistency and control to their environment.
