Ceph 5 Playground Lab

Welcome to the Ceph 5 Playground Lab, your dedicated environment for experimenting with Ceph storage solutions. This lab is designed to provide users with a hands-on experience in deploying and configuring Ceph clusters effectively.

Infrastructure Overview:

  • Ceph Monitors: Three powerful servers dedicated to monitoring the Ceph cluster. These monitors play a pivotal role in managing cluster membership, monitoring cluster health, and maintaining cluster maps.
  • Ceph Nodes: Three nodes optimized for Ceph storage operations. These nodes collectively form the backbone of the Ceph storage cluster, responsible for storing and managing data across the distributed environment.

Key Features:

  • Deployment Flexibility: With a dedicated set of servers and nodes, users can effortlessly deploy Ceph clusters tailored to their specific requirements.
  • Configuration Versatility: Explore various configuration options and fine-tune settings to optimize performance, resilience, and scalability.
  • Hands-On Learning: Gain practical experience in setting up, managing, and troubleshooting Ceph clusters in a controlled environment.
