== Introduction

This lab is organized to allow you to have a hands-on experience with analyzing the customer issues by running troubleshooting tools on must-gather.

The must-gather is the most critical bundle of data available when it comes to analyzing and solving customer issues with Red Hat OpenShift and many of its add-on Operators.
In this instructive, hands-on experience, covers a deep dive through some actual customer issues as you learn about the standard OpenShift must-gather and its product specific variants.
You will be introduced to tools and techniques that make looking at a must gather easier, faster and more efficient so you can help your customers with ease.

== Who Will Benefit Most from attending a lab?

Anyone with an OpenShift understanding who wants to learn about debugging issues or analyzing clusters with insights.

== Objectives

Attendees of this lab will

* Walk away with practical experience understanding and analyzing OpenShift must-gathers with the best available tools and methodologies
* Leverage insights to analyze clusters and make meaningful observations about them
* Review real customer issues in order to help them develop practical methods around data analysis
* Understand what to ask customers for, in order to expedite debugging and problem resolution
