In this lab, gain a deep technical understanding of managing Ansible Automation Platform (AAP) 2.x on Red Hat OpenShift through an intensive, hands-on troubleshooting experience. This interactive session takes you inside the AAP Operator, showing you how to interpret Operator logs and Custom Resource statuses to accurately identify the root causes of deployment and runtime failures.

You will work in intentionally misconfigured and failure-prone environments, diagnosing real-world issues related to persistent storage, networking, and resource contention using native OpenShift diagnostic and debugging tools. The lab emphasizes a practical, investigative approach to problem-solving rather than scripted fixes.

By the end of the session, you will have gained the hands-on experience and troubleshooting methodology needed to minimize downtime, improve operational confidence, and maintain a resilient automation platform running on OpenShift.
