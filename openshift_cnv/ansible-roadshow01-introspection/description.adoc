== Ansible Automation Platform Roadshow 01 - Introspection - Lab

Imagine you've just joined a company with a sprawling IT infrastructure—hundreds or even thousands of network devices spread across multiple data centers. The problem? No one has a clear picture of what's actually running. You don't know which operating systems are in use, what firmware versions your network devices have, or whether some critical systems are outdated and vulnerable. The documentation is sparse, and manually checking each device would take weeks, if not months.
