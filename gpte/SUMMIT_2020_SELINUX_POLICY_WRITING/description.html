Description: Writing your own SELinux policies doesn’t have to be a terrifying prospect! This workshop will walk you through the process of creating a custom policy for a source-compiled application, using the advanced tooling present in Red Hat Enterprise Linux.

Long Description: The SELinux policy workshop is meant for anyone who needs to create application policies for applications that lack them. Some previous experience with SELinux is expected, but you don’t have to be a master. Also, knowledge of RHEL and general Linux functionality is required. We are going to start with a short overview and then we’ll get into the lab as soon as possible. That is where we will spend most of our time.
