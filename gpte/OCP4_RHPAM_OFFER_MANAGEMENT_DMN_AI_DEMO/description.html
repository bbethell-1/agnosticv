Service "RHPAM 7: Offer Management - DMN AI (PMML) Demo”

Introduction
The following demo showcases the capabilities of Streaming and AI-Powered Business Decisions. Best offers are decided for the customers based on their historical context and Predictive models. Real-time events are evaluated against an intelligent model for best offers. This is then visualized on a Kibana Dashboard. The offer eligible transactions can also be be used to kick start a loyalty management case. The demo also includes a Quarkus based UI for narrating the story of how the offer can be sent to the customer.
