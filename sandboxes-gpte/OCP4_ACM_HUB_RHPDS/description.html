Description:

In this workshop for Red Hat Advanced Cluster Management for Kubernetes you will walk through these 5 major use cases:

  • Multi-Cluster Lifecycle
    • Centrally create, update and delete Kubernetes clusters across multiple private and public clouds.
    • Search, find and modify any kubernetes resource across the entire domain.
    • Quickly troubleshoot and resolve issues across your federated domain.
  • Policy Driven Governance
    • Centrally set and enforce policies for security, applications, and infrastructure.
    • Quickly visualize detailed auditing on configuration of apps and clusters.
    • Built-in CIS compliance policies and audit checks.
    • Immediate visibility into your compliance posture based on your defined standards.
  • Advanced Application Lifecycle
    • Easily deploy applications at scale.
    • Deploy applications from multiple sources.
    • Quickly visualize application relationships across clusters and those that span clusters.
  • Multicluster Observability for health and optimization
    • Work with Grafana to explore your cluster health leveraging the out of the box dashboards.
    • Create custom metrics and Dashboards in Grafana.
    • Navigate the out of the box optimization dashboards.
  • Multicluster networking
    • Leverage Submariner (Open Source Project) to connect multiple clusters together.
    • Explore and manage the networking resources for your clusters.
