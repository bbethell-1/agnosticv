=== Supporting Materials

Learn how to elevate your container development work with Podman Desktop Kubernetes is the premier platform for managing containers at scale. But, let's face it, diving into the world of Kubernetes and working with it as a developer can be intimidating, especially for those just starting their containerization journey. Don't let that stop you, and discover Podman Desktop. In this workshop, we'll introduce Podman and unveil Podman Desktop, an open-source GUI tool that streamlines container workflows and is compatible with Podman, Docker, and more.
