Welcome to our innovative workshop, where we delve into the future of retail technology! Join us in exploring "AI-Based Smart Retail: Event-Driven Ansible & ChatOps," a comprehensive session for professionals keen on integrating advanced technology into the retail sector. 

This workshop presents a unique opportunity to understand and apply a solution pattern developed for Globex, a fictional but highly progressive retail store. The workshop will walk you through Globex's modernization journey, showcasing how they have successfully implemented cutting-edge technologies for enhanced customer interaction and efficient product management. 

You'll learn about integrating Event-driven Ansible, Automation Controller, Apache Kafka, and ChatOps systems, forming a powerful infrastructure to handle customer feedback and product reviews in real time.

=== Key Takeaways

* Discover the role of Customer sentiment analysis in understanding client feedback
* Learn about Event Triggering using Event-driven Ansible and Automation Controller and how it revolutionizes message dispatch
* Understand the utilization of a Feedback System and Grafana Dashboard for visualizing product reviews and making data-driven decisions
* Explore ChatOps System Integration for executing real-time actions based on customer feedback

The workshop is structured to provide an in-depth look at each component of this integrated system, demonstrating how they work in synergy to enhance customer engagement and empower product managers with real-time decision-making capabilities. By the end of this session, participants will gain valuable insights into creating a similar system for their retail environments, leveraging AI, serverless architectures, and real-time data analysis for smarter retail management.
