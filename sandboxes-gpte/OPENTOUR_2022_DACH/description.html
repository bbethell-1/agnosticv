This catalog item deploys OpenShift with ServiceMesh, GitOps and Pipelines for the Open Tour 2022 Developer Track.

The environment is build for two sessions at the Red Hat OpenTour 2022 DACH:

  • Hands-on: From Zero to Hero! Pipelines combined with GitOps

    New technologies can be overwhelming, especially if you combine them. In this session, we break down the complexity into simple, easy, understandable steps. Let’s walk together through the journey from Zero to a Pipeline combined with the GitOps approach.

    After the Session, you have a solid understanding of OpenShift Pipelines and OpenShift GitOps in theory and practice. What helps to understand and adapt the techniques for your environment.

    Material:

    • Presentation

    Author: Robert Bohne

  • Hands-on: Using Service Mesh for Resiliency and Observability

    With the move to microservice architectures came the challenges of distributed computing. Although required patterns such as circuit breakers or client side load balancing could be solved with libraries like Netflix Hystrix or the Spring Cloud framework, infrastructure-related code still had to be written instead of being able to focus on the business logic. Service meshes now solve typical problems in the areas of resiliency, security, observability, routing and more. In this hands-on session, we’ll show you how to take advantage of service mesh capabilities and to get rid of infrastructure-related code in your microservices.

    After the Session you'll have a good understanding of what a Service Mesh is and how you can use it. Especially you'll be able to use the traffic shaping, observability and resiliency features of a Service Mesh.

    Material:

    • Presentation

    Author: Nikolaus Lemberski
