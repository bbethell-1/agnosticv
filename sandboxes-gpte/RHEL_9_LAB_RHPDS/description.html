Explore core and updated capabilities of Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL) 9.

This hands-on lab leads you through a mix of self-paced exercises covering core and updated capabilities of RHEL 9. Key topics include:

  • Ease of use:
    • Web console
    • YUM 4
    • "application-streams"
  • Security:
    • System-level disk encryption
    • Session recording with TLOG
    • "net-filter" and "nft-tools"
  • Performance:
    • Performance profiles with TuneD
    • Advanced tracing with EBPF
  • Management:
    • Gold image creation with image builder
    • Containers with Podman, Skopio, Buildah
    • Boot image management with Boom

Some exercises may not be available due to the deployment backend. Be sure to follow the directions and begin your journey to becoming a RHEL 9 expert.
