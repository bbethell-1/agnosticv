This lab was part of Red Hat Summit 2023. In this lab, we walk-through disaster recovery and business continuity strategies and demonstrate how to use OpenShift disaster recovery features to implement service availability at pre-defined acceptable levels following a disruptive incident.Reference to the presentation for understanding the strategies

Organizations running mission-critical applications on OpenShift need a business continuity plan for site or even regional disasters that goes beyond cluster failover or backup and recovery. Its implementation should provide data resiliency and take into account the application continuous deployment practices as a part of the overall enterprise GitOps strategies.

Products referenced in this lab

- OpenShift 4.12.x

- ACM 2.7.x & Submariner

- ODF 4.12.x and ODF RDR

In this lab exercise participants will understand how to failover an application requiring data persistence using Advanced Cluster Manager and OpenShift Data Foundation.

Participants will be able to:

- Understand some key strategies around Disaster Recovery and Business Continuity on OCP.

. Learn and understand how ACM (along with Submariner) and ODF Regional DR can be used to achieve DR on OCP.

. Understand how Continuous Deployment and DR work together.

This catalog item presents the regional disaster recovery with OpenShift Data Foundation Open Hybrid Cloud demo service.
