Perform tasks covering core and updated capabilities of Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL) 8.

  • Ease of use:

    • YUM 4
    • "application-streams"
  • Security:
    • System-level disk encryption
    • Session recording with TLOG

  • Performance:

    • Advanced tracing with eBPF
  • Management:
    • Gold image creation with image builder
    • Containers with Podman, Skopio, and Buildah

Some exercises may not be available due to the deployment backend. Be sure to follow the directions and begin your journey to becoming a RHEL 8 expert.
