Learn about Red Hat OpenShift Pipelines, a pipeline-building solution based on Tekton.

This catalog item provides an introduction to OpenShift Pipelines, a cloud-native, continuous integration/continuous delivery (CI/CD) solution for building pipelines using Tekton.

The content of this asset is aligned with the content provided at the OpenShift Pipelines scenario at Red Hat Interactive Learning Portal.

After provisioning completes, look for an email from Red Hat Product Demo System (RHPDS), and take note of the homeroom URL. The asset content is under the Labs project, and you share the homeroom Route URL with your attendees. To sign in, attendees need to provide a username and password. Available usernames correspond to the number of users requested when you ordered the environment:

  • Usernames: user1 ... userN
  • Password: openshift

If a user makes errors that make their environment unusable, they can log out and choose a different user by clicking the menu in the upper-right corner of the homeroom interface. They can also clear cookies.
