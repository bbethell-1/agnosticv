SSP OpenShift Demo

The NA App Platform SSP (Specialist Sales Person) demo is a foundational OpenShift Demo covering “quality of life” features. It will serve as a baseline script for future demos covering additional application platform features. Customization by SSPs to suit individual account/opportunity needs is encouraged.
