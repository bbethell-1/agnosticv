= GenAI POC/Workshop

Explore GenAI. In this immersive experience, you will have the opportunity to deploy and work with  AI models while utilizing various features of OpenShift AI.

Deployment time: 2 hrs.

NOTE: This is a very costly catalog item due to very high capacity GPUs, so kindly delete the environment once you complete your workshop or demonstration.

== Key highlights of this workshop include
* Exposure to Large Language Models (LLMs) and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

This workshop provides a glimpse into how AI/ML technologies can be applied to real-world business problems like GenAI. Please note, while the models and techniques used in this lab are illustrative of a prototype, they are not designed for a production environment.
