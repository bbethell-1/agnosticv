Red Hat Discovery is a lightweight, containerized scanner that identifies and reports on Red Hat technologies across your environment. In this demo, we demonstrate the capabilities of the Red Hat Discovery tool by guiding users through end-to-end scanning workflows. The session concludes with setting up and running scans across different products and environments, and generating comprehensive inventory reports. The goal is to showcase how Discovery simplifies asset visibility and helps organizations gain actionable insights into their IT landscape.

NOTE: This is a beta release and does not currently provide a guide. If you have feedback, see the support contacts at the bottom of the page.
