The Openshift 4 installation lab is a 4 hour lab design to help learners with installation tasks around setting up Openshift for the first time. Use of this lab should be associated with concurrent enrollments through the Partner OPEN learning portal or the internal Red Hat LMS.


NOTE: As of Sep 1st, 2021 the runtime of this lab will be shortened to 6 hours to match the length of the learning objective.
