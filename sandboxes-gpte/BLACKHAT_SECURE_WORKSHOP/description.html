Welcome to our hands-on Blackhat event workshop titled "Secure Your Container Pipelines: A Deep Dive into Tekton Chains Image Signing and Red Hat Advanced Cluster Security (ACS)." We aim to bolster your enterprise's software lifecycle security in this workshop, with a focus on securing your DevSecOps processes. The workshop will divide into two parts. First, we will explore Tekton Chains, an open-source project designed to ensure the security of your software supply chain by providing robust signing and storage options for your Tekton task runs. You'll learn hands-on how to sign images, store signed attestations, and audit your pipelines, all while understanding the best practices for secure, traceable, and auditable CI/CD pipelines. The second part will introduce you to Red Hat Advanced Cluster Security (ACS), demonstrating how to use this premier solution to achieve end-to-end security for your Kubernetes applications.
