Learn to deploy, configure, and use Red Hat® Quay on Red Hat OpenShift® Container Platform 4.

This workshop provisions a Red Hat OpenShift cluster with Homeroom and the Quay Operator installed. The content walks students through the deployment, configuration, and use of Quay on Red Hat OpenShift.

Products: Red Hat Quay, Red Hat Container Platform 4

This workshop is currently in the public beta phase. Please allow at least 24 hours for support, and deploy your workshop with this limitation in mind.

Specifications

Nodes: 3 leader, 2 worker, 1 User

Supporting Materials

Access: You have full admin access to the provisioned workshop cluster. The details you need to access your cluster are in the Access Guide.
