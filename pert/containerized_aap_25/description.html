This catalog item provides a RHEL 9.4 environment. This will be used to do the deployment of Containerized Ansible Automation Platform which runs the component services as podman based containers on top of a RHEL host. Containerized Ansible Automation Platform is the first step towards a more streamlined and improved platform management experience, incorporating with future vision and strategy.

What Content Is Covered In The lab?
  • Understanding of the Ansible Automation Platform's (AAP) features, architecture, and component.
  • Discover how to evaluate the infrastructure needs and set up the environment for the deployment of AAP.
  • Know the benefits of using the right Topology for the deployment.
Lab Guide
  • Basic Deployment of AAP 2.5
Environment Specifications
  • 1 x Red Hat® Enterprise Linux 9.4
  • 16 GB RAM and 70 GB Storage.
Duration
  • Provision of system takes Typically ~10 minutes
  • 60 minutes for the LAB
Audience

  • Developers
