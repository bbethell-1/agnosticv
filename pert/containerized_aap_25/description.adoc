=== Overview

This catalog item provides a **RHEL 10** and **OpenShift SNO Cluster** environment for learning and practicing Ansible Automation Platform (AAP) 2.7 deployment and fundamentals.

AAP runs component services as podman-based containers on top of a RHEL host and pods on top of Red Hat OpenShift, representing a streamlined approach to platform management that aligns with Red Hat's future vision and strategy.

=== What You Will Learn

* Understanding of Ansible Automation Platform's (AAP) features, architecture, and components
* How to evaluate infrastructure requirements for AAP deployment
* Benefits of using the right topology for your deployment
* AAP 2.7 deployment Hands-on practice

=== Environment Specifications

* **OpenShift SNO Cluster** - For operator based AAP deployment.
* **RHEL 10 VM** - For containerized AAP deployment.
