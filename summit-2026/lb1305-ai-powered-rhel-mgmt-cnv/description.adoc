Discover how AI transforms RHEL infrastructure management. This hands-on lab connects Red Hat Satellite, Red Hat Insights, and RHEL Lightspeed to large language models through Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers, enabling natural language interaction with your managed fleet. Instead of navigating multiple UIs or writing complex scripts, you query your infrastructure in plain English and let AI generate, explain, and execute remediation actions on your behalf.

== Detailed Overview

* *Satellite MCP Server*
** Connect an AI assistant to Red Hat Satellite via the Foreman MCP server
** Query your managed fleet in natural language — find hosts, check errata, review compliance
** Trigger remote execution jobs and patching workflows through conversational AI

* *Insights Integration*
** Surface actionable recommendations from Red Hat Insights using AI-assisted analysis
** Identify vulnerable hosts and security risks without manually navigating the Insights UI

* *RHEL Lightspeed*
** Use AI to generate, explain, and troubleshoot shell commands and Ansible tasks on RHEL hosts
** Apply AI-suggested fixes to broken services and misconfigurations directly from the lab guide
