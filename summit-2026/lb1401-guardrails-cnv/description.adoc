*WORKSHOP*: LB1401: Securing GenAI Applications with Guardrails as a Service

In this hands-on lab, learn how to secure AI applications at scale with TrustyAI 
by delivering guardrails as a service on Red Hat OpenShift AI. As organizations 
rapidly integrate generative AI into their applications, traditional security 
controls—such as firewalls, authentication, and encryption—are no longer sufficient 
to prevent threats like prompt injection, data leakage, and policy violations. 
These emerging risks can expose sensitive data, harm brand reputation, and disrupt 
compliance efforts. This lab teaches you how to build a modular guardrail architecture 
where all AI traffic passes through an orchestration layer that enforces security and 
compliance checks by default, with added monitoring and visualizations. 

Attendees will try how different detectors—such as prompt injection detection, 
content safety filters, language or business policy validation—can be combined and customized. 
By the end of this session, attendees will understand how to standardize, scale and automate AI 
security practices on Red Hat OpenShift AI and how to continuously test and evolve guardrails 
against emerging threats using open source tools. 

Are you ready to defend your application?
