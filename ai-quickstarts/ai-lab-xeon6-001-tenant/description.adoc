
Episode 001: Your first hands-on experience with AI inference on Intel Xeon 6 CPUs. Run an inference server in a container, send prompts via curl, and see how CPU-based AI works -- no GPUs required. ~20 minutes.

== What You Will Do

* *Pull and run* a vLLM inference server container with podman
* *Send prompts* to Granite models running on Intel Xeon 6 via MAAS/LiteLLM
* *Compare responses* across different model sizes (1B to 8B parameters)
* *Measure latency* and understand tokens-per-second on CPU inference

== Models Available -- Intel Xeon 6 CPU Inference

[cols="2,1,1,2",options="header"]
|===
| Model | Parameters | Hardware | Use Case
| granite-4-0-h-tiny-cpu | ~1B | Intel Xeon 6 (CPU) | Ultra-fast classification

| qwen25-3b-cpu | 3B | Intel Xeon 6 (CPU) | Classification, summarization
| phi3-mini-cpu | 3.8B | Intel Xeon 6 (CPU) | Complex reasoning
| granite-3-2-8b-instruct-cpu | 8B | Intel Xeon 6 (CPU) | Complex reasoning
|===

All inference runs on Intel Xeon 6 with AMX acceleration via MAAS/LiteLLM. $0/token.
